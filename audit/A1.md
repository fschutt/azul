top of that tag.

## Claims

Line numbers are those of the tag `audit-A1-structure`.

### Where things live since the structural commit (read this before you patch)

`layout/src/cpurender/raster.rs`
- `walk_items` (new): THE loop over a list's items. The full repaint (`render_display_list_with_state`),
  every damage rect (`paint_damage_rect`) and a view's child list (the `VirtualView` arm of
  `render_single_item`) all call it; they differ only in `WalkCull` (`InGroups` / `Damage(rect)` /
  `ActiveClip`) and in `bind_colors`. The damage cull (`test_bounds` against the rect, the scroll-offset
  subtraction) lives THERE now, once, not in `render_display_list_damaged`.
- `paint_damage_rect` (new): one pass = clear the rect, seed the stacks, `walk_items`. A band of a
  repaint is one call of it.
- `GroupGeometry::of` (new): the device box, the matrix `layer_h` about the frame's origin and the outer
  clip of an isolated group: the algebra that was the first half of `ReferenceFrameGroup::begin`, word for
  word. `begin` calls it and then opens the surface.
- `group_part_within(matrix, outer, below, surface, margin)` (new name of the body of
  `group_visible_part`, which is now `group_part_within(.., GROUP_VISIBLE_MARGIN)`).
- `render_single_item`: signature UNCHANGED. Its arms are where they were.
- `CpuRenderState`: two new fields, `paint_threads` and `retained`, and their builders.

New files: `cpurender/bands.rs` (thread budget, band planning, the thread scope), `cpurender/retained.rs`
(`RetainedFrames`), `cpurender/item_grid.rs` (spatial index over a list's items).

## Counters

All through `cpu_counters::CpuFrameCounters` (the window's frame report); A11 adds them to
`CpuCountersJson` (`layout/src/e2e/full.rs`) and the scenarios.

| field | counts | 0 means |
|---|---|---|
| `bands` | passes of the damaged walk that were bands of a larger rect | every rect was one pass |
| `band_threads` | threads started for bands | serial frame |
| `groups_empty` | isolated groups whose box lands nowhere: no surface | |
| `group_px_painted` | pixels of the part of each group surface a walk could paint | |
| `retained_hits` (existing, was always 0) | groups composited from a kept surface | |
| `retained_misses` | groups that could be kept and were painted | |
| `retained_invalidations` | kept surfaces dropped because what they show changed | |

`groups_opened` / `group_px` keep their meaning (groups with a surface, and the surfaces' pixels).
