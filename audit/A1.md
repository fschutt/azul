# A1: the CPU renderer (parallel, retained, tiled)

`pixmap.rs` only on top of that tag.

## Claims

First version (from the outline, before the full read). It is refined as the read goes on; the line
numbers are those of `audit/base`.

### Functions A1 rewrites or restructures (do not patch inside them; send the finding instead)

`layout/src/cpurender/raster.rs`
- `ReferenceFrameGroup::begin` / `ReferenceFrameGroup::finish`, `TransformGroup` and `TransformGroup::finish`
  (the group surface: allocation, the "lands nowhere" case, retained surfaces, text drawn through the matrix)
- `group_visible_part` (only its callers' use of the result; the inverse mapping itself stays: A2-cpu may patch it)
- `render_display_list_damaged` (the damaged walk: becomes "plan bands, paint each band")
- `render_display_list` / `render_display_list_with_state` (the full walk: same band entry)
- `render_single_item`: ONLY the arms `PushReferenceFrame`, `PopReferenceFrame`, `VirtualView` and the
  cull prologue. Every other arm stays where it is and as it is.
- `CpuRenderState` (struct: fields are ADDED, none renamed or removed) and its constructors
- `acquire_pixmap`
- `PooledTextRaster` and the three thread-locals next to it: only made per band thread (they already are
  thread-local); the body of `take` / `drop` stays

`layout/src/cpurender/compositor.rs`
- `execute_virtual_view_shift` and `ScrollShiftOutcome` (`present_extra`; the strip read from a retained surface)
- `collect_virtual_view_shifts` (only if the retained strip needs one more field of `ViewShift`)
- nothing else: `CompositorState` and its layer functions, `compute_damage`, `gpu_value_damage`,
  `window_value_damage`, `list_value_damage`, `scroll_shift_region*`, `execute_scroll_shift`,
  `execute_translate_blit`, `overlay_rects_after_frame*` are NOT claimed

`layout/src/cpurender/pixmap.rs`
- `AzulPixmap::new` (the 255-fill) plus a new zeroed constructor next to it
- `blit_pixmap_affine_clipped` (only its thread fan-out: one shared knob for the thread count)
- nothing else

`layout/src/cpu_backend.rs`
- `CpuBackend` (struct: fields added) and `CpuBackend::render_frame`

`layout/src/cpu_counters.rs`
- new counters; a way to add a band thread's counts to the frame's (`merge`)

`dll/src/desktop/shell2/headless/mod.rs`
- the CPU frame functions (what the renderer reports as present damage), not the present side (A10)

New files (skeletons in the structural commit): `layout/src/cpurender/bands.rs` (band planning, the thread
scope), `layout/src/cpurender/retained.rs` (retained group surfaces and their invalidation),
`layout/src/cpurender/item_grid.rs` (spatial index over a list's items).
