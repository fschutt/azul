## The architecture (for `CPU_RENDERING.md`)

1. A repaint is a set of disjoint damage rects; each is painted by passes (`paint_damage_rect`): clear, walk
   the list once, clipped. A rect of at least two bands' worth (200,000 px a band, at least 48 rows, at most
   8 MB of frame rows) is cut into bands of rows, two per thread, taken from a queue by up to
   `bands::max_threads()` threads (4, `AZ_CPU_THREADS`, `CpuRenderState::paint_threads`), the caller included.
2. A band paints in the frame's coordinates into a pixmap of the frame's size (zeroed allocation: only its
   rows are touched); its rows are copied in, its rect copied back into rows the thread alone holds
   (`split_at_mut`). No lock in a paint loop; positions are the single pass's, so are the pixels.
3. Shared read-only: lists (`Arc`), `FrameIndex`, resources, fonts, render state, kept surfaces. Per thread:
   glyph cache (kept between frames, dropped past 4,096 entries), rasteriser pool, box-shadow cache, counters
   (merged). Shared behind a lock: the frame thread's kept halos.
4. A walk steps over a reference frame that cannot paint (`FrameEntry::self_contained` + extent / landing
   test): O(frames + items that reach the clip) per pass instead of O(items).
4a. Grids (round 5, A1-07b, `item_grid.rs`): a list of more than 256 items is read once into levels (the top
   of the list, the inside of each scope) and their children (a leaf, or a scope from push to pop), each
   child with a box in list coordinates or none (frames, scroll frames, filters, and scopes that hold one:
   always visited). A level with more than 256 children has a uniform grid. A walk that enters a level
   takes ONE rect for it (`level_cull_rect`: its clip or damage rect moved by its offset, widened by the
   open text shadows' reach and a pixel), asks the grid for the children that meet it, and steps from one
   to the next; what it visits goes through the walk's own tests as before, so the painted items and the
   pixels are those of the walk that tests every item. Kept in the list's `FrameIndex` (dropped with the
   list), shared read-only by the bands. A list that is not a tree has no levels. `AZ_CPU_GRID=off`.
4b. Text ink (round 5, A3-core-26): a list carries, per text run, the run's ink relative to its first pen
   (`DisplayList::text_ink`, from the face's glyph boxes, grown by 1 px + 0.1 em), and everything that
   asks where a run paints asks the LIST (`visual_bounds_of(i)`): the walk's culls, the grids, frame and
   group extents (`GroupBounds`), the damage diff, the repair of a kept surface, the backdrop check of the
   `Text` arm. An item without an entry, or with an entry that is not its own (another glyph count),
   answers with the old estimate. The per-glyph 4 em reach inside the text functions is unchanged (it
   bounds AGG's integers, it is not the cull that trusts bounds).
5. Kept group surfaces (`retained`): the surface of a large isolated frame is kept with its painted boxes and
   composited again through the live matrix when its key matches; one surface of up to 192 MB, a second while both are at most 48 MB
   (`AZ_CPU_RETAINED_MB`). Read-only during a frame; `begin_frame` drops surfaces of lists no longer painted,
   `commit` merges what the frame's passes (and bands) painted.
5a. Kept opacity groups (round 5, A1-26): the pixels a BLENDED opacity group (opacity under 1, or a blend
   mode) paints on its cleared region are kept the same way, in a surface of the group's whole device box,
   and a later walk blends them at the live opacity with the pop's own loop (`blend_group_pixels`) and steps
   to past the pop (`FrameIndex::opacity_scope_at`: the pop's index and the keys bound inside; the group's
   own key is not in the key). Never kept: a group at opacity exactly 1 without a blend mode (its text may
   be LCD: another picture, and one that depends on the clip), a group under a text shadow of the list
   around it, inside another group, mask, filter or isolated frame, holding a view, under 256 x 256 px, or
   not self-contained. Bounded apart from the frames' surfaces: 8 pictures, 24 MB together
   (`MAX_FADE_ENTRIES`, `FADE_MAX_BYTES`); a replaced list drops them (no repair).
6. A group whose box lands nowhere has no surface; surfaces and scratch pixmaps come zeroed from the allocator.
7. The frame reports moves (`last_present_moves`) and the damage left after them.

## What bands do not scale (answers to A8 and A5)

- The band count is decided by what can be counted before painting: the rect's pixels (200,000 a band), its
  rows (48 a band), the frame's row bytes (8 MB a band) and the thread budget. Nothing about the content.
- LCD text: agg's `pixfmt_lcd` takes a process-wide mutex (A8 is finding out for how long). If it is held for
  a run's whole blend, LCD text does not scale: Berlin's settle frame (text 52 %, LCD 82 % of that = 43 %
  serial) gets 1 / (0.43 + 0.57 / 4) = 1.75x on four threads instead of about 3.5x; Tuebingen's (text 8 %)
  about 3.2x. Without the lock both scale with the bands. Text inside a zoomed picture is grayscale (no lock).
  The fix is in agg-rust-azul (the user's crate), not here; `AZ_CPU_THREADS=1` is the serial fallback.
- Kept halos under bands: ONE store, the frame thread's, shared behind a lock while the bands run
  (`share_cpu_halos`), not one per thread; a lookup is a lock and a hash probe per shadowed run. Its bound
  and eviction are A5's: `MAX_CPU_HALOS` 4,096 / 32 MB here; at a scale factor of 2 a halo has 4x the bytes,
  so the byte bound holds about 1,000 label halos of 150 x 40 px x 4 and Tuebingen's 1,567 evict each other.
  After the merge with A5 the CPU store should take A5's rule (a frame never evicts what it or the frame
  before used; 96 MB) and `share_cpu_halos` / `restore_cpu_halos` its type.

## Memory (answers to A9)

- Kept surfaces (A1-28): ONE of up to 192 MB is always kept, a second only while the two are at most 48 MB
  together. View of the whole window with 256 px of overscan: 2560 x 1440 at 1x: 3072 x 1952 px = 24.0 MB (two
  kept, 48.0 MB); the user's 1470 x 956 pt at 2x: 3964 x 2936 px = 46.6 MB (one kept); 4K at 2x: 186 MB (one).
  Resident is the painted part. Over the bound for one: not kept, said once on stderr.
- Kept opacity groups (A1-26): at most 8 pictures and 24 MB together, each the device box of a blended
  group of at least 256 x 256 px (a 300 x 200 pt panel at 2x: 0.96 MB; a window-sized overlay on the user's
  panel: 22.5 MB, one). Resident is what was painted. `AZ_CPU_RETAINED_MB=0` keeps none.
- Bands: a band's pixmap is frame-sized virtual memory, at most 8 MB of it touched (`MAX_BAND_BYTES`) plus the
  group surfaces of what lands in the band; at most `threads` alive at once; freed when the band is copied
  back. A zoomed frame painted in bands holds one lazily zeroed group surface per band until `commit`.
- Glyph caches: one per extra band thread (3), each dropped when it holds more than 1,024 entries (the
  window's own may hold 28,672). A band looking up the window's cache read-only first and keeping only its
  misses needs a `&self` lookup on `GlyphCache` (A8's to offer); not done.
- Box-shadow blur cache: one (16 MB bound), shared with the band threads (A1-25). CPU halo store: one, 16,384
  entries / 96 MB, over it only by what two consecutive frames use (A1-22).
- A9's table 2 (compositor): the root layer as a second window buffer and the layers rebuilt per full repaint
  are gone for every list without a blur filter (A1-21: `CpuBackend::compositor` is `None`). For a list with
  a blur filter both remain as they were: layers are allocated from the list on every full repaint and the
  root layer is a window-sized buffer, dropped at the next resize or by `release_caches`.
- `previous_gpu_values` is moved into the frame's render state and back (A1-16), not cloned.
- Not done: the root compositor layer as a second window buffer and the layers re-made per full repaint
  (A9 table 2). See Open.

### Designs for what is open

- **A1-07b grid** (BUILT, 525d647fc; the design it was built from): per level (the list's top level; the inside of each non-leaf frame) a uniform grid over the
  bounds of its direct children (a leaf item: its visual bounds; a scope that is self-contained and holds no
  frame, scroll frame or view: the union of its content; a frame: unbounded, always visited). A pass asks the
  grid for the children that touch its clip mapped into the level's space, gets their start indices sorted,
  and `walk_range` jumps from one to the next (children it skips are self-contained, so the stacks are right).
  Built lazily next to `FrameIndex` for levels with more than 256 children.
- **A1-26 kept opacity groups** (BUILT, f2f2706da; the design it was built from): key as `SurfaceKey` with the group's `PushOpacity` index; the surface is the
  group's cleared region painted alone; a hit blends it at the live opacity (the pop's loop). Same rules 1-7.
  What it needs: (1) `FrameIndex` entries for opacity scopes (push, pop, extent = `opacity_extent`, the keys
  bound inside: the same `FrameNest` / `FrameInner` gathering with `ScopeKind::Opacity` as a root); the
  group's OWN opacity key is not part of `values` (it is the value the hit applies). (2) In the `PushOpacity`
  arm, before the region is cleared: `retained_candidate`-style test (self-contained scope, size over
  `MIN_SURFACE_PX`, no scroll frame or view inside), `lookup(key, region)`; on a hit push
  `MaskEntry::Opacity` with the kept pixels as the group's content and let the walk step to the pop
  (`walk_range` already steps over frames by index; the same jump). (3) At the pop of a miss: `offer` the
  group's pixels before they are blended. (4) Text inside is grayscale already (`inside_blended_group`), so
  a kept group and a fresh one have the same text. Test: a fade from 1.0 to 0.2 in five steps, kept == fresh
  at every step, `retained_hits` 4.
- **A1-43 filters as isolated groups of the flat walk (retires the layered compositor)**: today a
  `PushFilter` list of flood / composite alone is an isolated group of the walk (`FilterGroup`: the box inside
  the clip set aside, painted on transparent pixels, filtered, composited back). Every other filter needs the
  group's pixels BEYOND the pass's clip (a blur reads `radius` px around each pixel), which is why it has a
  layer. The design: a filter group gets a SURFACE of its own, as a turned reference frame does
  (`ReferenceFrameGroup`): (1) the surface is the group's box grown by the filters' reach (blur radius x 3,
  0 for colour filters), and the part of it painted is `clip` mapped into the box and grown by the reach
  (`group_part_within(.., margin = reach)`); (2) the walk paints the group into it with the clip stack reset
  to that part and the offset set to the surface's corner (what `ReferenceFrameGroup::begin` does for the
  identity matrix); (3) the pop runs `apply_layer_filters` on the painted part and composites it under the
  pass's clip. A backdrop filter is the same with the surface seeded from the target's pixels under the box
  (grown by the reach, clamped to the target) instead of cleared, and the group's own items painted over the
  filtered copy. Bands: a band's private pixmap holds its rows only, so a backdrop filter with a reach needs
  the rows `reach` above and below copied into the band's pixmap (`paint_band` copies rows in already: widen
  by the largest reach of the list, kept in `FrameIndex`). With that, `full_repaint_needs_layers`,
  `allocate_layers_from_display_list`, `render_layers`, `composite_layer_recursive`, `painted_over_later` and
  the whole of `Layer` go, a window with a blur repaints its damage, and a filter inside a view's list works.
  Why it is (a) and not a patch: it removes a painter with 3,000 lines and 150 tests of its own, and every
  layer rule those tests pin (seeded scroll layers, opacity layers, in-place frames) has to be shown to be
  held by the walk first.

## Round 5: what the verifier's first runs found, by hand

Four failures of pixel identity / of the grids' test, each worked out by reading and replaying the numbers;
the commits have the algebra. In short, for the next reader of a "damaged != full" report:

- **A row of a scaled picture a level off after a pan (A1-62).** View at (48, 40) 304 x 200, picture under
  `translate(12, 0) scale(3)`, pan from offset (37, -60) to (-121, -155): pixels moved by (-158, -95), two
  strips composited. Box (c 0, r 1) of the picture: x = 48 + 48 - 121 = -25 .. 107, y = 40 + 24 + 144 - 155
  = 53 .. 137; its upper edge's middle row (y = 54) is where the source position is a whole texel:
  `s = (d + 0.5 - shift) / 3 - 0.5`. Computed as two products, `s` came out `k` in one frame and `k - 1e-15`
  in the other: texel `k` at weight 0 against texel `k - 1` at weight 255. 107 - 48 = 59 px.
- **A turned label's sides inside a strip (A1-63).** AGG rounds a geometric cut to 1/256 px; the cut edge
  then runs from the cut to its far vertex, off by up to 1/512 px at the cut and less further on, enough
  to flip a coverage level here and there. A cut must never depend on the pass.
- **A column next to a damage rect (A1-64).** The image of a rect through a matrix is not what a
  composite paints: bilinear taps reach half a surface pixel past the surface's device box. Damage through
  a composited frame is the image of the rect grown by 2 px in the frame's space, plus 1 px.
- **An item "painted" under an empty clip (A1-65).** `intersect_clips` of two clips that do not meet is a
  rect of no area at the far corner; a closed overlap test against it accepts whatever holds that corner.

Candidates the reports named that were NOT the cause: bands, kept surfaces, direct turned text, the damage
of an earlier value change in pre-pan coordinates, A1-60 (`GroupBounds` for nested frames), the rounding
of a damage rect's near edge (the rect was right; the paint reached past it).

## Round 6: the spinner's damage (A1-66), by hand

The verifier's failing pre-existing test: `a_visible_spinners_frame_damages_only_its_own_rect`: "a spinner
frame damaged 66.065796 x 66.065796 @ (-9.0329, -1.032898), outside the spinner's own 32 x 32 @ (8, 16)".

**The DOM** (`layout/src/widgets/spinner.rs`, `ring_arc`; the flat theme's ring): the container (32 x 32,
fades in), in it the ARC (a frame, turns 0 -> 900 degrees per 2 s, linear), in it the WINDOW (a frame,
turns with the arc's tail: 0 degrees over the first second) which holds the BODY (a frame, turns with the
head: from -135 degrees), and two caps (frames). Every part is a full-size 32 x 32 node turned about the
box's centre (24, 32), clipped to its shape by a clip path. No `overflow: hidden`, no clip around the
turning parts: nothing bounds the damage to the 32 px box but the geometry.

**The number.** At the test's frame the arc stands at 21.9 degrees, the window at 0, the body at about
-134. `gpu_value_damage_of`, for the arc's changed key, took the arc's content from
`FrameIndex::extent_with` - a BOX: the arc's own box united with the box around the body's turned box,
16 x (|cos 134| + |sin 134|) = 16 x 1.414 = 22.63 from the centre - and put it through
`FrameStack::map` with the arc's matrix: moved out by 2 px (`COMPOSITE_REACH`, A1-64), turned by 21.9
degrees, boxed, grown by 1 px: (22.63 + 2) x (cos 21.9 + sin 21.9) + 1 = 24.63 x 1.3007 + 1 = 33.03
from the centre: 66.07 px, at (24 - 33.03, 32 - 33.03) = (-9.03, -1.03). Exactly the report. The true
image of the body's box is its corners through BOTH turns: turned by -134 + 21.9 = -112.1 degrees, 16 x
(0.376 + 0.927) = 20.84 from the centre.

**Which change.** Not one change alone: `extent_with` as the content (A2-cpu-04, `66af92a36`: before it
the content was the items where they are LAID OUT, 16 from the centre, which missed what an inner frame
moves) gives the box around the turned body; A1-64 (`6ec61ce06`) adds the 2 px before the turn and the
pixel after it. Before both: 16 x 1.3007 = 20.8 (inside the test's old bound of 25, and too small for
anything an inner frame moves out of its box). `FrameStack::map`'s own doc already says why a box must
not go through a turn ("a spinner turned inside a turned frame damaged 58 px for its 32 px"): it takes
the corners through all the frames AROUND an item - but the frames INSIDE the changed frame came in as a
finished box.

**The fix.** The content of a frame goes through its matrix as POINTS: `FrameIndex::hull_with` (the
corners of the frame's own extent, and of every frame inside it through that frame's matrix, level by
level; `HullPoint = (x, y, reach)`), `FrameStack::map_hull` (those points through the frame's own matrix
and the frames around it; `map`'s rule point for point; for the four corners of a rect it IS `map`, bit
for bit, so a frame without frames inside it is damaged exactly as before). A box is taken once, at the
end. What a composited frame INSIDE the hull's frame paints past its content (A1-64's reach: 2 px on both
axes in the frame's own space) is carried as a radius per point: + 2 x sqrt 2 before the frame's matrix,
stretched by the matrix's largest singular value. That part is new damage, not less: `extent_with`'s box
had no reach for the inner composites at all (it held their rim only where a box around a turned box is
loose). Used at: the value damage of a frame (`gpu_value_damage_of`), of an opacity group that holds
frames (`opacity_hull_with`; the region the group is blended over stays `opacity_extent_with`'s box), and
the list diff's changed frame pair (`compute_display_list_damage_impl`). NOT changed: `extent_with`
itself and everything that sizes a SURFACE or a region from it (`raster.rs`, `present_layer.rs`): a
surface is a box in its frame's space, and its size is part of kept surfaces' keys.

At the test's frame the arc's rect is now 27.27 from the centre (the body's corner at -89 degrees, (0.39,
-22.62), moved out to (-1.61, -24.62), turned by 21.9 degrees: y = -23.45; with the body's own 2.83:
26.27; grown by 1): 54.5 px. The body's own rect (its key changed too; through `map`, unchanged by this
commit) is 27.05 from the centre.

**The test's expectation, decided.** Its bound was "the box and D/4 + 1 = 9 px a side" (25 from the
centre: the circle the corners sweep, 22.63, and 2.4 px). That cannot hold for any damage that includes
A1-64's rim: the BODY's own damage rect alone - one 32 px box through three composited frames - reaches
up to 22.63 + 3 x 2.83 + 1 = 32.11 from the centre (each frame's 2 px on both axes is 2.83 along the
diagonal), and A1-64's rim is needed (the pixel (64, 138) of the a2_cpu sequence). The spinner does not
clip its turning parts to the box, so there is no clip to intersect the damage with. The bound now says
what is true whatever the angles: `D (1/sqrt 2 - 1/2) + 3 x 2 sqrt 2 + 1` = 6.63 + 8.49 + 1 = 16.11 px
past the box. The box taken twice fails it at this frame (17.03 past the box) and by far at others (up
to (16 x 2 + 2) x 1.414 + 1 = 49 from the centre). The unit tests pin the exact numbers.

**Left as it is, on purpose** (each is over-damage on a rarer path, never under-damage):
`windowed_structural_damage` (a structural change of a list: the frame's `extent_with` box through the
frames around it), `node_groups_on_screen` (the animation cull: a larger box only keeps an animation
ticking that is just off screen), the overlay boxes of the shifts. They take the box where the points
would be tighter for a turn in a turn.
