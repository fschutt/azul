## 4. What was built

**A. The inline-block's baseline and a percentage height that computes to auto.**
- ONE rule for CSS 2.2 10.5, `sizing::percentage_height_computes_to_auto(height, cb_definite)`, asked
  by every place that decides a box's height: `calculate_used_size_for_node` maps such a height to
  `auto` (a block then starts from the 0 placeholder, not the estimate that became the floor of
  `apply_content_based_height`'s max); cache.rs Phase 2.5 (the old `cache::is_percentage_height` is
  gone); `fc::measure_atomic_inline` sizes such an inline-block by its content (it kept
  `tentative_size.height` for every non-auto height); and `sizing::height_is_auto_for_children`, the
  one rule of `cache::prepare_layout_context` and `fc::layout_bfc` for "this box gives its children no
  height": auto, or a percentage that computes to auto - except table boxes, whose heights the table
  algorithm decides (CSS 2.2 17.5.3; a cell's percentage child keeps resolving against the cell).
  `MultiValue::as_exact` (borrowing twin of `exact`) for the MultiValue call sites.
- `fc::inline_block_baseline` (beside `first_line_baseline`): CSS 2.2 10.8.1 as Blink reads it - the
  last line box in the normal flow, searched from the last in-flow child up (out-of-flow boxes skipped);
  a table answers nothing (`LayoutTable::InlineBlockBaseline` = -1); a child whose overflow is not
  visible answers with its bottom margin edge; a flex / grid child with its first baseline. With
  `UnifiedLayout::last_line_baseline` (text3): the last line's baseline where `position_one_line` put
  it. `measure_atomic_inline` uses it for inline-blocks (inline-table / -flex / -grid keep their own
  layouts' baselines). Probe: Chrome / prebuilt `ib` y 14 / 0, `ref` 32 / 36 (last line), 14 / 64
  (table skipped).

**C.** `layout_cell_for_height` (block branch): the content height is the final layout's
`overflow_content_size` at the column width, nothing else; the cell's own `height` is read by
`cell_specified_border_box_height` (added on 2026-10-01, after the `measured` term it replaces).
Probe: `<td><table style="width: 80%">one two</table></td>` cell 24 / 42.

**E1.** `perform_fragment_layout`: a line that holds glyphs counts its whole line box (top and bottom,
`glyph_line_box_top` / `line_box_extent`) in the IFC's extent, like a line a lone `<br>` ends already
did. A line of ONLY atomic inlines is still measured by its items (decision 6.3).

**F.** `measure_intrinsic_widths`: in the collapsing white-space modes a leading collapsible space is
not folded and a line measures to its last item that is not one (the breaker's strip_leading /
strip_trailing). Internal spaces and the fold order are unchanged.

**G.** `apply_l2_visual_reversal(line_items, base)`: an Object (U+FFFC, a neutral) takes the direction
of the clusters on both sides where they agree, the paragraph's otherwise and at a line's edges (UAX 9
N1 / N2), and joins the run it resolves into. LTR paragraphs without RTL text: unchanged.

## 6. Decisions taken (unattended)

1. **D not changed.** The macOS `sans-serif` order lives in the external crate (rust-fontconfig 5.0.0 from
   crates.io, `FcFallbackConfig::os_defaults`), and changing it changes every app's `sans-serif` text
   on macOS. For Chrome parity: put "Helvetica" before "Helvetica Neue" in the MacOS `SansSerif` list
   upstream (one line, a rust-fontconfig release), or override it once at startup with
   `FcFontCache::set_fallback_config`. Also seen: a family that is missing with NO generic after it
   falls back to sans-serif in azul, to the standard font (Times) in Chrome.
2. **E2 not changed.** azul's `<hr>` (top border only, `width: 100%`) is a deliberate UA choice pinned by
   core's `hr_line_comes_from_the_border_not_from_height`; the standard's is a 1px inset border on all
   four sides with `width: auto` (2px tall, two-tone). 1px per `<hr>` stays inside the measure's
   tolerance. A look decision for the user.
3. **Atomic-only lines keep their items' height** (E1's exception; LAYOUT7 noted it too): Chrome adds the
   strut's descent below an inline-block / image alone on a line (a 10px box makes an 18px line), which
   moves every icon button of the apps - it needs a look pass. Note: today such a line is also
   internally inconsistent when its box is shorter than the strut's ascent (the item sits at y 4, the
   IFC is 10 tall: 4px overflow).
4. B is XML8's (parser); I did not add a layout workaround.
5. The probe and helper scripts stay in the worktree's ignored `target/mailref8/` (as LAYOUT7's did).
