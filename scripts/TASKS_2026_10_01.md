from an `.app`), and nothing reminds while the app is closed - a missed reminder shows at the
next start.

## 2. Engine changes (RED first)

- **G1 `CallbackInfo::get_node_attribute` read only the attribute list**, while
  `Dom::with_contenteditable` / `with_tab_index` (every text field, every keyboard stop) keep
  those as FLAGS - so it said a text field is not contenteditable and an app could not ask
  "is the focus in a text field" (AzTasks' single-key shortcuts). Fixed at the root: after the
  attributes, the flags answer as HTML spells them (`contenteditable` "true", `tabindex` "0" /
  "-1" / n). RED `81898c7b6` (tests appended at the end of `layout/src/callbacks.rs`,
  `node_attribute_flag_tests`, through `roving::test_support::fire`), GREEN `003181699`.
- **G2 a tree view could not be a drop target** - the plan's "drag a task onto a sidebar list"
  (AzMail: a message onto a folder; AzDrive: a file onto a folder) had no hook.
  `TreeView::with_on_node_drop` (`TreeViewOnNodeDropCallbackType(RefAny, CallbackInfo, node:
  usize)`): every row accepts a drag over it and reports the node a drop lands on; the
  navigation pane wires it while the app listens and reports
  `ShellNavigationPaneEventKind::NodeDropped (group, index)` (appended last). RED `49d53f845`
  (`tree_view::drop_tests`, `navigation_pane::drop_tests`, appended at the ends of the files; RED
  by compile), GREEN `9205c10f3`. **Touches another task's file minimally**: AzShells' exhaustive
  match in `examples/azul-shells/src/lib.rs` names the new variant. **MAIL2's AzMail** must do
  the same if it matches `ShellNavigationPaneEventKind` exhaustively. `tree_view.rs` is also
  edited by MAIL2 (the badge): different hunks (callback types after the toggle's, a struct field
  before `theme`, `RowContext`, the end of `render_rows`, a test module at the end) - expect a
  textual merge, no semantic one.
