## What the LOOK showed (prebuilt aa59b2d84, headless, flat / flora x light / dark)

- EVERY shell app (AzSetup, AzShells, AzCalculator, AzReview, AzMaps): the UA body margin (8px) and a
  content-high `<body>`: the shell sat in a band at the top of the window, white canvas below it (dark mode
  too), panes collapsed (AzShells S5: tree / content / preview had no height), AzSetup's wizard button row cut
  off or pushed out of the window on the components page. Root cause: every app wrapped its `ShellThemeScope`
  in a hand-styled `Dom::create_body()`. FIXED once in the engine: `ShellThemeScope::body()`.
- AzCalculator: Cmd/Ctrl+C after a mouse click on the keypad never reaches the app (engine, see "Not fixed");

  engine bug, see below).
- AzReview: a hard-coded light palette (no dark mode, no flora), hand-made div buttons, sessions written with
  `std::fs` into the temp dir from five callbacks.
- AzMaps: a hand-rolled `#2b2b2b` header with blue div buttons, a recoloured Titlebar, no theme / mode, nothing

  theme (and the debug server's `set_theme`) left the page in the other theme; a hand-made title row.

## api.json (module `shells`, class `ShellThemeScope`) - one method

- `body(self: value) -> Dom`, doc: "The scope as the window's body: the root an app's layout() returns. The body
  fills the window - no UA margin, the full height - and the scope grows in it, so the shells' panes take the
  window." fn_body: `object.body()`.

Every app calls it through the generated `azul` crate, so the apps do not build until it is in api.json and the
bindings are regenerated. Nothing else public changed in azul; azul-appkit is a plain Rust crate (no api.json).

## Decisions

- One window-filling helper on the shells (`ShellThemeScope::body()`), not CSS strings in seven apps.
- `from_const_str` in the generator, not per-app helpers: every app follows the prefix ruling with it.
- AzBuilder keeps its empty `<body>`: it is the builder's canvas - the first builder document is what the window
  shows (`BuilderDocument::from_styled_dom`), so a shell or body styles there would leak into every new design.
  Documented in examples/azul-builder/src/lib.rs.
- AzWidgets' Flat / Flora segment is the app theme (one switch with `set_theme`), named "Theme" (naming ruling).
- AzMaps' pins pane is a column of Buttons (a TreeView's click reports a flattened index, a list of places does
  not need a tree); the viewport is kept after ~30 idle ticks of the sensor timer (one write per rest, not per
  frame of a drag).
- AzReview saves after every kept stroke as before, now off the UI thread; overlapping saves are serialised.

## DEDUP twins found and removed

- AzSetup's command-line parser (twin of `azul_appkit::args`) - deleted.
- AzShells' and AzSetup's E2E debug-server clients (twins of `scripts/azlin_e2e.py`) - both use the shared one.
- AzWidgets' three per-section `keep` copies - one `crate::keep`.
- The settings page's theme / mode callbacks vs. an app's own theme buttons - `kit::choose_theme` / `choose_mode`.
