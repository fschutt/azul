## 1. What was built, per item

### Cell width (prerequisite of almost every table test)
- `fc::cell_specified_border_box_width` reads a cell's definite `width` as a border box
  (content-box: W + padding + border; border-box: max(W, padding + border)); percentages are
  left to the column distribution (TABLE-A).
- `fc::cell_width_contribution(min, max, W)`: with W the cell contributes
  `max(min-content, W)` as BOTH its minimum and maximum (a `width: 50px` cell of prose stays
  50px and wraps, a 1px cell of one long word is the word's width).
- Used by `measure_cell_widths` (the layout's column measurement) and by
  `sizing.rs::calculate_table_intrinsic_sizes`. The intrinsic pass's "definite preferred size"
  block leaves cells alone (TABLE-A's hunk, verbatim).

### 1. Collapsing border model (CSS 2.2 17.6.2)
- `analyze_table_structure` is now THE grid placement, reading only the tree and the cascade
  (`&LayoutContext`, `pub(crate)`): rows with their row group (`row_groups`), cells with their
  slots, column boxes (`<col>`, also a bare `<col>` under the table, a childless `<colgroup>` as
  its own columns, `span` from `AttributeType::ColSpan`) and column groups. `visibility:
  collapse` on columns / column groups now marks `collapsed_columns` (it was a no-op).
- `resolve_collapsed_borders` -> `CollapsedBorders` (one `Option<BorderInfo>` per horizontal /
  vertical grid edge). Participants per edge: the two cells, the rows, the row groups at their
  first / last row, the columns and column groups at their boundaries, the table on the outside.
  `collapse_edge`: `hidden` anywhere suppresses the edge, `none` / zero width takes no part,
  then `BorderInfo::resolve_conflict` (width, style, element), left / top wins a full tie. No
  edge inside a spanning cell.
- `apply_collapsed_table_borders` (called first in `sizing::calculate_intrinsic_sizes`, so the
  intrinsic pass, the column measurement and the final layout all see it; also the paged
  pipeline): a cell's border = half of the widest edge on each side; the table's border = half
  of the widest edge on each side (browsers take the inline sides over all rows too -
  border-collapse-006 needs that); the table's padding = 0. Written into the hot `box_props` AND
  the cold `unresolved_box_props` (a parent `layout_bfc` re-resolves its children from the cold
  props). Idempotent; an empty collapsed table keeps its own border.
- Painting (`display_list.rs`): `paint_collapsed_table_borders` reads the same resolution and
  the grid lines off the cells' border boxes (`table_grid_lines`, unknown lines interpolated):
  one strip per run of equal edges, centred on its line; vertical strips between the row lines,
  horizontal ones after them and over each joint by half the widest vertical edge there; a solid
  winner is a rect, any other style a one-sided `Border` item (dashes, dots, double, bevels go
  through the normal border renderers, CPU and GPU).
- Layers 2 / 3 (`paint_table_column_backgrounds`): column group and column backgrounds under the
  cells of their columns (each cell's rect cut to the columns' extent - no paint in the spacing).
- Layer 1 of every table: shadows, all background layers and, in the separated model, the
  table's own border (`paint_table_items` returned before the generic painter, so a separated
  table never painted its border and only its background colour). Fixed first inside
  `paint_table_items` (`aa2fa8076`), then re-done as TABLE-A did it - the table's ordinary box
  painting with the border suppressed when collapsed, `paint_table_items` after it - with A's
  hunks verbatim (`bc087a71f`).

### 1b. Separated model (CSS 2.2 17.6.1)
- The spacing is resolved once (0 in the collapsing model / for anonymous tables), by
  TABLE-A's `resolve_table_border_spacing`, taken verbatim (`51c16a9df`; mine was the same
  function under another name); `TableLayoutContext::h_spacing` / `v_spacing` carry it to the
  span widths and the fixed layout.
- The columns share the content box minus `(columns + 1)` spacings (the table size adds them back).
- `TableLayoutContext::cell_span_width`: a spanning cell is laid out AND placed with the spacing
  between its columns; a rowspan cell's rows count the spacing between them.
- `sizing.rs` table intrinsic sizes add `(columns + 1)` / `(rows + 1)` spacings.
- `empty-cells: hide` (`DisplayListGenerator::cell_is_hidden_empty`): no background, no border
  for an empty cell in the separated model (`fc::is_cell_empty`, `fc::get_empty_cells_property`
  now `pub(crate)`).

### 2. Spans
- Column measurement: one-column cells first, then spanning cells by increasing span (they
  were measured and spread in document order, before the columns' own cells were in).
- `distribute_cell_width_across_columns(.., inner_spacing)`: the inner spacing comes off first,
  what the columns lack goes to them in proportion to their max-content widths (evenly when all
  are 0); max never ends below min.
- `sizing.rs::calculate_table_intrinsic_sizes` was a second column algorithm (twin): its own
  placement (rowspans ignored - a cell under a rowspan landed in the wrong column) and its own
  even spread. It now reads `fc::analyze_table_structure`, spreads with
  `fc::distribute_cell_width_across_columns`, adds the spacing, and grows the last row under a
  taller rowspan cell.

### 3. Fixed layout (CSS 2.2 17.5.2.1)
- Only for a table with a definite width (`table_has_definite_width`); `width: auto` + `fixed`
  is automatic layout, as in browsers.
- Step 1 `<col>` widths (`column_box_widths`), step 2 first-row cells (`fixed_layout_width`: a
  length as `cell_specified_border_box_width`, a percentage of the columns' share as content
  width plus padding and border), spanning first-row cells split over their open columns after
  their inner spacing, step 3 the rest share what is left equally, step 4 a table wider than all
  fixed columns gives them the extra in proportion to their widths.
- A cell is exactly as wide as its column even when its padding is wider (content width
  clamped at 0 in `layout_cell_for_height`).
- In automatic layout a `<col>` width is its column's width too (17.5.2.2 step 2):
  `min = max(min, W)`, `max = min`.
- Not done: "overflow clipped by cells" (the generic `overflow` property applies; nothing
  table-specific was needed for the tests).

### 4. Heights and baseline (CSS 2.2 17.5.3)
- `cell_specified_border_box_height`: a cell's `height` (content-box or border-box) is a minimum
  for its box in `layout_cell_for_height` - for a cell of text too.
- `specified_length_height` of the row box is a minimum for the row.
- `stretch_rows_to`: a table's own `height` (border-box aware) gives its rows what they lack, in
  proportion to their heights (evenly when empty).
- Only cells with a baseline alignment (`is_baseline_aligned`: everything but top / middle /
  bottom) set the row baseline; the row grows to hold each shifted cell; a baseline cell of
  BLOCK content is moved down too (`position_table_cells`), and `first_line_baseline` finds the
  first line at any depth, offset by where each box sits.
- Percentage heights on rows / cells stay ignored (no basis without a definite table height).

### 5. Min-content of cells (coordinator item)
- The `cell_is_ifc` early return in `layout_formatting_context` (7534be8c7) is removed, as the
  parent did on main.
- `measure_cell_content_width` tells the paths apart explicitly: a cell that is one inline
  formatting context (`cell_is_inline_formatting_context`) is measured with `layout_ifc` under the
  min-/max-content constraint (longest word / longest line) and its measurement line layout is
  dropped; the final pass (`layout_cell_for_height`) is untouched. A cell holding a block (or
  loose text plus a block) keeps the generic measurement, where the block child is sized at its
  min-content under the min-content constraint (no change needed).
- `prose_cells_wrap_inside_a_220px_table`: the `#[ignore = "OPEN for TABLE-B ..."]` the parent
  added on main is not in my base - remove it at the merge.
