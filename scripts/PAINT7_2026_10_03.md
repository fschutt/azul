# PAINT7 - paint order, compositor, hit test, animation publishing (wave 7, 2026-10-03)

Branch `wt/paint7` from `2e55eef06`. Nothing compiled (house rule); every edited `.rs` parses
(`rustfmt --check`, no parse errors). Every behaviour change is a RED test commit, then the fix.
The root causes of items 5-7 were found by running the PREBUILT binaries headless (capped runner, one
at a time; probe script in /tmp/paint7, not in the repo) and reading their display lists, hierarchy
dumps, `get_animations` and `AZ_ANIM_DEBUG=1` screenshot transform maps.

## 1. What was built (brief items)

| # | item | root cause | fix |
|---|------|-----------|-----|
| 1 | a positioned box painted before an earlier transformed sibling | the display list painted a `position: relative / absolute` box with `z-index: auto` in its parent's IN-FLOW walk (CSS 2.2 E.2 step 4): under every later in-flow box and under every transformed / translucent sibling, even an earlier one | `display_list.rs`: such a box is a `StackingContext { positioned_box: true }` entry of its stacking context, filed in tree order with the level-0 stacking contexts (`file_into_context`); its own positioned descendants and stacking contexts follow it in the same list (E.2 step 8: they belong to the parent context); it pushes no stacking context; the in-flow walk skips it. One rule: `node_paints_as_positioned_box` next to `node_establishes_stacking_context`. A dragged box is painted last at step 8 too. |
| 1 / 4a | ...and the CPU compositor still put the transformed box over it; a sheet under an earlier scroll layer (MEETDRIVE6 7a) | the layered compositor composites a child layer after ALL of its parent's own items: whatever the list paints later, over a scroll frame / opacity group / transformed box, came out UNDER it | `compositor.rs`: `painted_over_later` - a group the parent paints something over LATER (before the parent's end, overlapping where the group's pixels land; it follows the frames around the group as it leaves them, counts a later scroll frame's content as its window and a later reference frame's as its moved content) is not promoted and is painted in place, where list order is paint order. Blur and backdrop filters keep their layers. |
| - | (found on the way) the INCREMENTAL CPU frame ignored transforms | `render_single_item` kept the reference frames' matrices on a stack nothing read: every damage rect of every CPU backend repainted a transformed box at its LAYOUT place (mid-slide ghosts; a lasting transform ghosted wherever damage touched its layout box). This also made in-place painting of a transformed box impossible | `raster.rs`: `MaskEntry::Transform(ReferenceFrameGroup)`: a 2-D translation is folded into the scroll-offset stack (exact, culling still works); any other matrix is an isolated `TransformGroup` (box set aside and cleared, content clipped to it like the compositor's layer, composited through the matrix about the frame origin into the clip open around the push; nothing culled inside). One matrix classification shared with the layer builder: `is_identity_2d` (now pub(crate)), `translation_2d`. |
| 2 | the hit tester ignores the animation transform channel | the display list opens a reference frame from the CSS channel OR the animation channel; the hit tester's chains read `css_transform_keys` only and both hosts' `resolve_tf` read `css_current_transform_values` only | ONE rule: `GpuValueCache::reference_frame_of` / `painted_transform_of` (core/src/gpu.rs) and `GpuStateManager::painted_transform_of`; the display list's two copies of the lookup are gone; the hit tester, the e2e runner's and the dll's `resolve_tf`, the a11y snapshot and `LayoutWindow::painted_transform_of` (was `css_transform_of`) ask it. |
| 3 | a CSS-id image registration may stay invisible on X11's GPU path | `apply_content_change(ImageById)` answered `RebuildDisplayList` ("rebuilt, send it" - the dll's convention, documented at `ProcessEventResult::ShouldUpdateDisplayListCurrentWindow`) WITHOUT rebuilding, unlike its clip-mask and node-style arms; X11 resends a dirty list as it is | `window.rs`: the arm regenerates every laid-out dom's display list itself. |
| 7 | an `overflow: hidden` span clips its glyphs after ~5 px (AzCalendar "Lunch with Ana"); WIDGETS7: three one-hour events show only their colour | NOT the display list (its clips are right): the compositor. A clipped box is a scroll frame, hence a layer, clipped at composite time by the intersection of EVERY open `PushClip` - clips opened outside an enclosing scroll frame (its window's space), inside it (its content space) and outside the parent layer (the grandparent's space) alike; composite then moved the mix by all in-place frames. Scrolled to the working day, the lunch block kept a few pixels of its time line and the other blocks' text was clipped away entirely | `compositor.rs`: `Layer::wrapping_clips` - only the clips opened inside the parent layer, each with the in-place frames IT was opened in; `render_layers` resolves `static_clip` moving each by its own frames' offsets; composite uses it as it is (outer clips reach the layer through the clip composite hands down, as before). |
| 6 / 5 | incremental relayout garbles (AzTasks after Cmd+2: duplicated bold text, rows over rows); SHEETSHOW6's overlapping sorter thumbnails, ribbon labels after a tab switch | seen on the prebuilt AzTasks: the SETTLED frame is right; mid-slide, 87 nodes slide and 70 of them inside another sliding node, all with the same ABSOLUTE FLIP offset; the display list nests their reference frames and every renderer composes nested frames, so a child moved twice, a grandchild three times | `window.rs`: `LayoutWindow::published_flips` - each slide is published relative to the nearest sliding ancestor whose frame paints its frame (per the display list's paint structure: a stacking context's frame wraps every descendant, an in-flow or positioned box's only its in-flow walk); general for scale (frames act about their box origin). A child moving with its parent is published still. Keyframe tracks are a node's own motion and unchanged. |
| 4d / 5 | pieces of the closed backstage stay painted over the ribbon after a theme + mode switch (MEETDRIVE6 7d); SHEETSHOW6's ghost labels after a theme switch | seen on the prebuilt AzDrive: Options > Appearance > Flora / Flat, Escape, `set_theme flora` + `set_mode dark` WITHOUT waiting: settled, with no animation in flight, the screenshot's transform map still held 15 animation-channel values (-658 px, -649 px ...); the back button sat in the window corner, the search box was gone. `tick_animations` released a slide's value only when the slide FINISHED (through `anim_key_to_node`); a rebuild mid-slide rebuilds that map and `drop_unplaced` drops the slides it cannot place - their values stayed for good | `window.rs`: `release_undriven_animation_values` keeps the animation channel to exactly the nodes of the slides in flight and the live keyframe tracks; after every tick, on the idle early return, and right after `finish_reconciliation` drops the unplaced slides. |

## 3. api.json

No change. Everything new is Rust-only: `GpuValueCache::{reference_frame_of, painted_transform_of}`
(core), `GpuStateManager::painted_transform_of`, `cpurender::{MaskEntry::Transform, ReferenceFrameGroup,
TransformGroup}`, `cpurender::Layer::wrapping_clips`; crate-private: `node_paints_as_positioned_box`,
`compositor::{is_identity_2d, translation_2d}`, `LayoutWindow::{published_flips,
release_undriven_animation_values, painted_transform_of}` (the last renamed from `css_transform_of`).
