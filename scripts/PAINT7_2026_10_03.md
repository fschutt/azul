# PAINT7 - paint order, compositor, hit test, animation publishing (wave 7, 2026-10-03)

Branch `wt/paint7` from `2e55eef06`. Nothing compiled (house rule); every edited `.rs` parses
(`rustfmt --check`, no parse errors). Every behaviour change is a RED test commit, then the fix.
The root causes of items 5-7 were found by running the PREBUILT binaries headless (capped runner, one
at a time; probe script in /tmp/paint7, not in the repo) and reading their display lists, hierarchy
dumps, `get_animations` and `AZ_ANIM_DEBUG=1` screenshot transform maps.

## 1. What was built (brief items)

## 7. Risks / behaviour changes to watch

- Item 1 moves every `position: relative / absolute` box with `z-index: auto` to step 8 of its
  stacking context (browser order). Widgets that relied on the old order (a relative box painted under
  a LATER in-flow sibling) now paint over it; a scroll container whose content holds relative boxes
  now reopens its frame around each (split frame -> painted in place, no layer). Tests that pin a
  display-list order of positioned boxes may need updating - they pinned the wrong order.
- `painted_over_later` scans the parent's later items for every candidate layer: O(groups x items) on
  a FULL frame (first frame, structural change, screenshot). Fine for today's apps; a 10k-item list
  with hundreds of promotable groups is the case to measure. A false "painted over" only costs a layer.
- The dll headless `repaint_only` still rebuilds a dirty list (HEADLESS6): for `ImageById` that is now
  a second rebuild, harmless.
