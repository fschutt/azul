# VIDEO8 - the video leftovers (report, 2026-10-03)

Branch `wt/video8` from `45c6bf98b`. Nothing was compiled with cargo (house rule). Every touched
`.rs` file parses (`rustfmt --check`, no new hunks against HEAD). What could be checked without a
build was: the pure modules and the new dll files were type-checked standalone with
`rustc --emit=metadata` (no binary; the dll files against small stand-in `azul_core` /
`azul_css` / `libloading` crates); the AudioToolbox Opus calls were replayed from Python
`ctypes` against this Mac's AudioToolbox; the rate controller, the RGB -> NV12 table, the FFT and
the echo canceller were modelled in Python and the tests' expected numbers checked against the
models (all under `/tmp/video8/tc`, not committed).
