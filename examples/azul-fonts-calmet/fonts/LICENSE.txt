Calmet UI (CalmetUI-Regular.ttf, CalmetUI-Italic.ttf)

Traced from the azul author's own digitisation of a public-domain print,
Augustin Calmet's "Commentaire litteral sur tous les livres de l'Ancien et
du Nouveau Testament" (1732), and made fit for a user interface by the same
author. The fonts are part of this crate and distributed under its licence
(MIT, see the repository's LICENSE).
